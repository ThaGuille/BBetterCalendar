# u3 - replay waits for the screen, not for the recorded pauses

Date: 2026-11-13

## What changed

- `bbadb.py replay` defaults to `--min-settle 0 --max-settle 0` (no recorded pacing); `--min-settle 400 --max-settle 4000`
  restores the old pacing.
- `START` runs `am force-stop <pkg>` before `am start` (`run_step`, so replay, record and run-steps; also `_steps.ps1`).
- `WAIT n` immediately before `TAP`/`ASSERT` is not slept: the next step polls (0.5 s pause) up to n ms for its node,
  then the usual 4-try retry budget. A WAIT before any other verb still sleeps.
- Consecutive `ASSERT`s (SHOT between them allowed) share one uiautomator dump (`cache` in `find_node`); a node missing
  from the cached dump takes a fresh dump and retries as before. Any other step drops the cache.
- Last line of replay: `PASS: '<s>' (<n> steps, no crash) in <t> s` / `FAIL: '<s>' had <k> failing step(s) in <t> s. See above.`
- `replay.ps1` is now a thin wrapper over `bbadb.py replay` (user decision d1).
- Extras needed to make the device check pass: `ASSERT ... expect~=a|b` alternatives (bbadb.py and `_steps.ps1`);
  `mv-u11-projects` asserts `7 de 12|7 of 12` / `8 de 12|8 of 12`; `seed_progress_db.py` deletes the walk's own
  `Reunion` events (they piled up on day 14, filled the agenda and scrolled the "+" button out of view on later runs).

## Consumers of the PASS:/FAIL: line (grep of .claude/**, CLAUDE.md, docs, recordings, scripts, tests, no worktrees)

Only `bbadb.py` and `replay.ps1` print or parse it; `test_bbadb.py` now asserts its format. SKILL.md and ui-tester.md
mention "PASS / FAIL" loosely, no parsing. No other consumer found.
